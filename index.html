Features

Everything you need
in one app

RouteBuddy is packed with features that make shared commuting effortless, safe, and rewarding.

🗺️
Live GPS Tracking
Track your driver in real-time on the map. Know exactly when they arrive and follow the route live.
💸
Instant Wallet Payments
Top up your wallet with JazzCash or EasyPaisa. Pay for rides automatically — no cash needed.
💬
In-App Chat
Message your driver or passenger directly inside the app — no need to share phone numbers.
✅
Verified Drivers
Every driver submits their CNIC, selfie, and driving licence for admin review before going live.
⭐
Ratings & Reviews
Rate drivers and passengers after every trip. Build trust with a verified community of riders.
🌿
Real CO₂ Tracking
See exactly how much carbon you've saved based on actual trip distance — not a rough estimate.

How it works

Simple for everyone

Whether you drive or ride, getting started takes less than 2 minutes.

🧍 For Passengers
1
Search a ride
Enter your pickup and destination. Browse rides sorted by time, price, and rating — or rebook a previous trip in one tap.
2
Book & invite friends
Request your seat and invite friends to split the fare. Each person pays independently — no manual splitting needed.
3
Track, chat & rate
Watch your driver live on the map, share your location in chat, and rate with stars, emoji, and an optional tip.
🚗 For Drivers
1
Get verified
Submit your CNIC and driving licence. Our admin team verifies you within 24 hours so you can start earning.
2
Offer a ride
Set your route, departure time, seat count, and price. Passengers find you automatically and can book instantly.
3
Earn & withdraw
90% of each fare lands in your wallet instantly. Request a withdrawal to JazzCash or EasyPaisa anytime — minimum PKR 100.
