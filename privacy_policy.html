🛡️ Overview

RouteBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") operates the RouteBuddy mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the RouteBuddy app, including passengers and drivers, in Pakistan.

By using RouteBuddy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

💡 In simple terms: We collect only what we need to make carpooling work safely — your location to match rides, your identity to keep the community safe, and payment info to process fares. We never sell your data.

⚙️ How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide and improve the RouteBuddy service:

  • Matching rides — Your location and route are used to find available rides near you.
  • Booking & payments — Your account details are used to process bookings, wallet transactions, and fare splits.
  • Safety & trust — Identity verification helps ensure all users are genuine and accountable.
  • Communication — In-app chat and push notifications keep drivers and passengers connected.
  • Ratings & feedback — Ratings improve the quality of the community.
  • Eco tracking — CO₂ savings are calculated from your trip distances to promote sustainable travel.
  • Customer support — Account data helps us resolve disputes and assist users.
  • Analytics — Aggregated, anonymised data helps us improve app performance.

📍 Location Data

RouteBuddy requires location access to function. Here is exactly how we use it:

  • Ride search — Your location is used to find rides near your pickup point.
  • Live tracking — During an active trip, your location is shared with the other party (driver sees passenger pickup; passenger sees driver position).
  • Foreground only — We access location only when the app is open or during an active ride session. We do not track your location in the background after a trip ends.

✅ Your control: You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. However, the app requires location to match rides — core features will not work without it.

💳 Payment Information

RouteBuddy uses an in-app wallet system. Here is how payment data is handled:

  • Your wallet balance and transaction history are stored securely in Firebase Firestore.
  • Card numbers and sensitive payment credentials are never stored on our servers — they are processed directly by JazzCash or Safepay.
  • All wallet top-ups and fare deductions are logged as transactions visible to you in the app.
  • Withdrawal requests are processed manually by admin and credited to your provided account details.

🔔 Push Notifications

We send push notifications to keep you informed about your rides:

  • Booking confirmations and cancellations
  • Ride status updates (driver accepted, trip started, trip completed)
  • Fare split requests and responses
  • In-app messages from your driver or passenger

You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings. You will still see notifications inside the app when it is open.

🗓️ Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services:

  • Account data — Kept until you delete your account.
  • Ride history — Retained for 2 years for dispute resolution and safety.
  • Transaction records — Retained for 5 years for financial compliance.
  • Identity documents — Deleted within 90 days of verification approval or rejection.
  • Chat messages — Retained for 1 year, then automatically deleted.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

🔒 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict database access so users can only read/write their own data.
  • Identity documents are stored in Firebase Storage with private access rules — only admins can view them.
  • Passwords are managed by Firebase Authentication — we never store raw passwords.
  • FCM tokens are regularly refreshed and cleared on logout.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

👶 Children's Privacy

RouteBuddy is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 18 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.
